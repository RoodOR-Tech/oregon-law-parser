# Reviewed ORS 696.370 amendment chain

This case covers two expressly successive amendments within **one act**, SB 1513
(2026 Oregon Laws chapter 76), rather than inferring an order between separate
acts. It is a reviewed preview, not an official edition or complete legal history.

The 2026 amendment table, PDF page 4 (RS26-T-4), lists ORS 696.370 amended by
chapter 76 sections 1 and 2. Both rows were visually reviewed before evaluating
the session parser. Their frozen identities and table provenance are in
`../2026-amendment-table-chain.json`.

Both pages of [chapter 76](https://www.oregonlegislature.gov/bills_laws/lawsstatutes/2026orlaw0076.pdf)
were visually reviewed, including both complete amended bodies, the section 2
heading expressly referring to section 1, and the operative/effective clauses:

| As-of date | Applicable preview | Effect within this reviewed history |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2026-03-30 | None | Original 2025 ORS row |
| 2026-03-31 | Section 1 | Remove original subsection (5); renumber (6) to (5) |
| 2027-06-30 | Section 1 | Section 2 remains pending |
| 2027-07-01 | Section 2 after section 1 | Add the name restriction as subsection (6) |

Section 4 takes effect on passage; the final page prints March 31, 2026.
Section 3 expressly defers section 2 amendments until July 1, 2027. The effective
date remains March 31, 2026 for both plans; their operative dates differ.

`696370-base.json` preserves the canonical section row from the source-pinned
[2025 ORS chapter 696 HTML](https://www.oregonlegislature.gov/bills_laws/ors/ors696.html).
It was generated through `ors_text.decode_markup`, `parse_chapter`, `build_rows`,
and `build_ors_relational.build_section_rows`; no manual body or offset changes.
The row's catchline and original subsections (1)-(6) were compared with the
printed section 1 baseline and its bracketed deletions.

The two `2026-c76-s*-enacted.txt` files are independently extracted complete
enacted bodies, visually checked against the PDF. PDFium preserved curly quotes;
only discretionary line-break markers, running header/footer, bracketed deleted
text, and whitespace were normalized. Their hashes were frozen before running
the session parser and agree with both proposed bodies. The successor's frozen
base-row hash covers the exact first-version body in the original row envelope.
Original offsets and credits are not assigned to the derived preview body.

Unit tests check these full texts, all four date boundaries, reversed input
order, unchanged original base, stale predecessor rejection, and missing second
clause evidence. The full-session CI gate independently verifies both exact
operative amendment references against the acquired chapter 76 parser output
and builds all four boundary snapshots using that actual verification report.
